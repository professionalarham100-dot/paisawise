Expo QR for iPhone

iPhone does not need a separate “scanner app” for Expo: use the Camera app and point it at the QR below. Paste the connection string from your PC (terminal or http://localhost:8093).

How to get the URL
1. Run: npx expo start --port 8093 --tunnel
2. Open http://localhost:8093 on this PC — copy the exp://... link.
3. Paste here → Generate QR → on iPhone open Camera and scan this white square.
4. Tap the banner → opens in Expo Go.
